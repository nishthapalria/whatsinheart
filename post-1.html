Entry No. 1

The Art of Ignoring People Who Deserve It

Toxic people are energy vampires. Stop giving them a buffet. Start giving them dry toast.

No butter. No reaction. Become so damn bland they choke on their own drama.

1. The Grey Rock Method: But Make It Indian

Mission: become the human equivalent of unseasoned lauki. No flavor. No nutrition. No reason to come back for seconds.

Why it works: toxic people feed on reaction. Tears, anger, defending yourself — they love it. "Hmm, okay" — they hate it. They'll go find a new person.

2. Your New Personality: 404 Emotion Not Found

Aunty

"Still not married?"

You

"Hmm." Sips coffee.

Cousin

"You've gotten fat."

You

"Noted." Say it confidently, don't explain.

Ex

"I miss you" — text at 2am

You

Seen. No reply. Ever.

3. The Art of Becoming NPC-Level Boring

Toxic people want drama. Your job is to be the loading screen.

  1. The Info Diet. They ask "How's the job?" You say "Good." Not "Good, got promoted, team loves me." Just "Good." Period. Starve them.
  2. The Enthusiasm Tax. Match their energy with -100%. They trauma dump. You: "That's tough." Flat tone. Like you're reading a grocery list.
  3. The Subject Change Jutsu. "When are kids coming?" → "Did you see tomato prices?" Derail. Confuse. Bore them into submission.
  4. The Broken Record. "You've changed." → "Maybe." "Why so rude?" → "Maybe." You're not Alexa. You don't owe explanations.

Pro tip: toxic people hate when they're not the main character.

4. Why You Feel Guilty: Uninstall That

You were raised to be nice and respect elders.

Reality: your kindness is not a 24/7 customer service helpline. You're not obligated to attend every argument you're invited to.

Translation: "But they're family" is not a contract that says please drain me. You don't set yourself on fire to keep them warm. You become a rock. Rocks don't burn.

5. What They'll Try When You Go Rock Mode

They'll escalate. Because you cut off supply. Expect:

  • Bait: "Wow, you're so arrogant now." — fishing for reaction.
  • Victim Card: "After all I did for you…" — cue violins.
  • Flying Monkeys: sends your mom to ask "Why are you hurting them?"
  • Final Boss: "Fine, I won't talk to you either." — the dream outcome.

Your move: don't flinch. A rock doesn't negotiate with the weather. Rain harder, idc.

6. The Sarcastic Kill Shot: Putting Them In Their Place

Use this sparingly. This is not for daily use. This is for when they've crossed 47 lines and your rock needs to drop one boulder.

Conditions: only deploy when 1) you're safe, and 2) you're done.

Say it calm, bored, with eye contact. Zero smile.

"That's an interesting opinion. I'll give it the exact amount of attention it deserves."

Pause. Walk away.

Why it works: no shouting, no gaalis. Just a verbal Uno Reverse that says I see you, I'm not playing. Toxic people hate a mirror. Be the mirror. Then be a rock again.

Final Ruling: Be So Boring, You're Invisible

This isn't about being cold. It's about being selectively warm. Your energy is expensive. Toxic people are broke. Stop giving discounts.

Master the art of ignoring and you master peace. You don't win by fighting. You win by not showing up to a fight they're having with themselves.

So next time they poke: don't be fire. Don't be water. Be a rock. Rocks don't get invited to drama. Rocks don't get opinions. Rocks just… exist. Unbothered.

And that's the most realistic flex there is. Because in a world addicted to reaction, no reaction is the final rebellion.
