The Crunch Factor: Rajasthani Chili Bites (Panko Style)

Forget the heavy besan batter of a traditional mirchi bada. This gives the Rajasthani classic an American tadka, using panko breadcrumbs for a lighter, shatteringly crisp finish.

The Essentials

  • The peppers: 10–12 jalapeños, slit and deseeded
  • The stuffing: mashed potatoes, cumin, amchur (mango powder), salt
  • Upgrade: add shredded cheddar for a molten center
  • The crust: a three-step station of seasoned flour, cornstarch slurry, and panko breadcrumbs

Quick Instructions

  1. Stuff & slice. Fill the chilies with the potato mix. Slice into 1-inch rounds or nuggets — this maximizes the crunch-to-chili ratio.
  2. The double crunch. Dredge in flour, dip in slurry, roll in panko. This creates an airy, bistro-style crust.
  3. Cook. Air fry at 200°C for 8–10 minutes with a light oil spray.

The Signature Dip

Ditch the chutney. Mix mayo, sriracha, and lime for a desi-aioli that cuts through the heat and complements the crispy texture perfectly.

Pro-tip: too spicy? Soak deseeded peppers in cold salt water for 10 minutes before stuffing to mellow them out.
