Entry No. 2

To the Millennials Who Are Exhausted, I Am Proud of You

Not "I need coffee" tired. Soul tired. The kind that lives in your bones from carrying everyone else's expectations since 1995.

You're tired of being the hopeless romantic. Tired of being the good child who translated report cards, WiFi routers, and emotional trauma. Tired of chasing validation from parents, bosses, and Instagram likes like it's a full-time job. Tired of living your parents' dreams because "we sacrificed so much" was the invoice they stapled to your birth certificate.

You did all of it. And then you did the impossible.

1. You Took a Stand When "Stand" Wasn't in the Family Dictionary

You said no to the rishta. You said no to "adjust kar lo." You said no to "what people would say" and chose therapy instead. You were the first generation to tell your mom: "Maa, that's trauma, not sanskaar." And lived to tell the tale. Brave AF.

2. You Went to War With Your Own Brain

Nobody talks about this part. You inherited anxiety, generational guilt, and "be strong = be silent." Then you had to unlearn it while paying rent in a city your parents still call too far.

  • You worked on your traumas in 45-minute therapy slots between Zoom calls.
  • You learned boundaries from Instagram reels and then practiced them on people who taught you boundaries don't exist.
  • You fought patriarchy at home before you could fight it at work. Before 9am, you'd already survived three microaggressions from Dad about late marriage.

That's not healing. That's combat. And you did it with no manual.

3. You Cancelled "Society" Before It Was Cool

You cancelled "settle down by 25" like it was a Netflix subscription. You cancelled the idea that your life is a community project. The kind of guts our parents call zidd. We call it self-respect.

4. But God, Aren't We Tired

Because here's the secret: breaking cycles is back-breaking work.

You had to be the family therapist while being the family disappointment. You had to earn money to buy freedom from people who said "we don't care about money." You had to parent your parents through your own boundaries while reparenting yourself.

No wonder you have no energy left.

5. Please Rest Now

You don't have to be the strong one anymore. You already did the work. You said the hard things. You paid the therapist. You chose the hard, lonely, correct path when everyone said adjust.

So now? Please log off.

Take the nap. Be the "bad child" who sleeps till 11. Be the person who chooses herself for once. Be the disappointment who books a solo trip and doesn't send pictures to the family group.

You earned rest. You deserve softness. You deserve a life that doesn't feel like a rebuttal to your childhood.

To every millennial reading this: I'm so proud of you.

Proud that you didn't become your parents. Proud that you didn't repeat the cycle. Proud that you learned "no" in five languages because your family only understood guilt. Proud that you're tired, but you're free.

You didn't just chase dreams. You killed nightmares. Generational ones.

So put your feet up. Order the expensive dessert. You fought the war. Now enjoy the peace.

You're not lazy. You're not selfish. You're done. And done is the most underrated achievement there is.
