Entry No. 7

Why Some People Are Just Dumb and Sweet

Not stupid. Dumb. There's a difference. Stupid is a choice. Dumb is a lifestyle, and somehow it's adorable.

You know them. The human golden retriever. Zero street smarts, infinite heart. They'd give you their last fry and also get scammed by it. Here's why these sweet dumbasses exist and why we'd literally die for them.

1. Their Brain-to-Heart Ratio Is Off

The science: their heart is a 500-watt speaker. Their brain is on airplane mode.

How it shows up: walks into glass doors but holds it open for six people behind them.

Translation: they operate on vibes, not facts. And the vibes are immaculate.

2. They Skipped the Cynicism Update

Most of us downloaded Trust Issues v4.2 after 2016. These people? Still running on factory settings: everyone is nice.

Evidence:

  • Tells the Uber driver their entire life story. Volume: loud.
  • Gets excited about Mondays. Mondays.

Why we love it: in a world of red flags and soft launches, they're a green flag waving in a hurricane. Dangerous for them. Healing for us.

3. Street Smarts: 0. Snack Smarts: 100

They can't read a room, but they can read you.

Sweet: remembers you hate coriander and picks it out of your bhel puri one leaf at a time.

The formula: IQ drops twenty points when logistics are involved. EQ jumps two hundred points when someone's feelings are involved. It's the dumb tax we're willing to pay.

4. Their Survival Skill Is Being Liked

Smart people have plans. Sweet-dumb people have people.

Case study: they locked their keys in the car. Again. But the locksmith gave them a discount because they are nice. They burned pasta. The neighbor brought them biryani just because.

Darwin missed this: in the wild, they'd be eaten. In society, they're fed. The ecosystem protects them. Why? Because they once cried at a dog food commercial. That's why.

5. The Dumb Is Strategic (Sometimes)

Plot twist: some of them know.

The play: act dumb, stay happy. If you don't understand taxes, no one asks you to do them. If you don't get passive-aggression, it can't hurt you.

Result: they've weaponized cluelessness. You're stressed about inflation. They're stressed because the cloud looks like a bunny. Who's really winning?

Tell: when it matters, they suddenly deliver. Your dumb friend who can't spell definitely will throw hands if someone's rude to you. The dumb is selective. The sweet is constant.

6. Why the Universe Keeps Them Around

  1. They're emotional support humans. Your anxiety? They don't understand it, but they'll sit with you until it passes. With snacks.
  2. They make you feel smart. You explain GST for the fourth time. They nod. You feel like a genius. Co-dependent? Maybe.
  3. They're proof we haven't all gone cold. In the age of "read 2:04pm," they still double-text to say saw this and thought of u.

How to Protect Your Local Sweet Dumbass

  1. Don't let them online shop alone. That $200 "manifesting" candle is not it.
  2. Translate the world for them. "No, 'let's do lunch' doesn't mean lunch."
  3. Don't exploit the sweet. They'd give you a kidney. Don't ask.
  4. Hype them back. They told six people about your promotion. Be their PR team.

Final verdict: some people are dumb and sweet because the world needed balance. For every calculated, hyper-aware, five-step-plan person, there's a friend who forgets their head but remembers your coffee order.

They won't fix your problems. They'll sit in the problem with you, make it ten percent funnier, and offer you half a biscuit.

And honestly? In 2026, that's the most radical kind of intelligence.

P.S. If you read this and thought "omg that's me" — it is. We love you. Please don't text your ex. Forward this to your smart friend instead so they can explain it to you.
