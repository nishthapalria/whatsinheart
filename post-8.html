Entry No. 8

Can We Admit It? The Mascara-Lash Extension Industrial Complex Is A Scam.

I can't be the only one done. Done with "clean girl" mascara that looks like you cried in the rain. Done pretending this is self-care.

Done with $200 lash extensions that make you look like a Bratz doll in bad lighting.

It's maintenance. And the math ain't mathing.

1. The Mascara Lie We All Bought

Mascara was supposed to be the 30-second fix. One swipe, you look awake!

What we got:

  • Panda eyes by 3pm
  • Flakes in your contacts
  • Four different tubes in your drawer because "this one doesn't clump" — spoiler: it does
  • Twelve minutes every night rubbing your eyes with coconut oil like you're trying to erase a tattoo

2. So You Got Lash Extensions. Babe, Why?

Let's talk. You walked out of the salon looking like Bambi. For three days.

What did you gain? Mascara gave you clumps. Extensions gave you commitment issues and a new car payment. You still don't look like "yourself but better." You look like you're wearing someone else's lashes.

3. The Two Minutes You "Saved" Cost You Your Face

Let's do the math you're avoiding:

Mascara

2 min/day = 12 hours/year + $40

Extensions

2 hours/month at the salon, and you can't wash your face properly = 24 hours/year + $3,000

What you could've done instead: sleep. Literally just sleep twelve more hours this year.

The truth: nobody ever said "she'd be prettier if her lashes were longer." They said "she'd be cooler if she stopped canceling plans because of her fill appointment."

4. The Radical Act Of… Just Having Eyelashes

Here's a concept: what if your lashes just… existed? No serum. No lift. No tint. No $60 lash curler that "changed my life."

I stopped wearing mascara for a month. You know what happened? Nothing. No one staged an intervention. My eyes didn't disappear. I did, however, stop finding black smudges on my pillowcase.

Extensions girls, try this: take them off once. Look in the mirror. Those are your lashes. They're fine. They've been fine. The salon just gaslit you into thinking "fine" was a problem.

The bottom line: beauty isn't supposed to be a second job.

If mascara is ruining your day, drop it. If lash extensions are running your calendar, burn them.

You don't owe the world big eyes. You owe yourself two minutes back. And $3,900.

The hottest look this year? Touching your face without fear. Rubbing your eyes when you're tired. Crying at The Notebook without texting your lash tech first.

Wash your face. Go to sleep. Your lashes will be there in the morning. And if they're not dramatic? Good. Neither is life.
